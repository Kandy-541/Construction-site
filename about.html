Who We Are

MAKIAS CONSTRUCTION is Cameroon's premier supplier of construction and fabrication materials. Specializing in cement, steel iron rods, premium roofing sheets, accessories, and custom glass fabrication, we serve clients ranging from individual builders to large industrial projects.

With offices and delivery services operating across key Cameroon regions like Yaoundé, Douala, Buea, Bamenda, and Bafoussam, we guarantee swift delivery and unmatched quality control.

Our Fabrication Excellence

Beyond supplying raw building materials, we operate state-of-the-art aluminum and glass fabrication workshops. We build sliding doors, frosted glass partitions, kitchen cabinets, guard kiosks, and custom podiums using heavy-duty materials tailored to Cameroon architectural environments.
